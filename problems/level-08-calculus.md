## Problem 8.1

**First derivative!**

### Question

What is the derivative of  x^3?

<details>
<summary>Click to reveal answer</summary>

### Answer

3x^2

### Explanation

Power rule: d/dx(x^n) = n*x^(n-1). Here n=3, so 3x^2.

</details>
