## Problem 10.1

**Cyclicity of digits!**

### Question

What is the last digit of 7^2024?

<details>
<summary>Click to reveal answer</summary>

### Answer

1

### Explanation

Powers of 7 cycle: 7,9,3,1 (period 4). 2024 mod 4 = 0, so last digit = 1.

</details>
