## Problem 3.3

**Difference of squares!**

### Question

Factor:  x^2 - 9

<details>
<summary>Click to reveal answer</summary>

### Answer

(x - 3)(x + 3)

### Explanation

Difference of squares: a^2-b^2 = (a-b)(a+b) with a=x, b=3.

</details>
